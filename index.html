04

Research

Technology is not treated as neutral infrastructure, but as a social field that can be observed, questioned and reclaimed.

Earoptica connects media art, ecological transformation, public space and digital self-determination.

05

About

Earoptica ’27 is a city-wide festival for media arts in Ghent.

The project brings artists, cultural institutions, researchers and citizens together around technological, ecological and societal transformation.

06

Lab

Earoptica Lab is the experimental layer of the project: browser works, interface studies and prototypes developed alongside the festival.
